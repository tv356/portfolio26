EXPERIMENT 01

AI + ACCESSIBILITY =

We redesigned Google Arts & Culture to be more inclusive for low-vision users. Our solution replaced immersive scrolling, added voice-based assistant and soundscapes to evoke emotion. This project won 1st place at the CodePath Design Challenge out of 500+ submissions.

EXPERIMENT 02

AI + CONTENT CREATORS =

Iris uses AI to detect PII and offensive content in TikTok videos before they go live. We combined LLMs, audio analysis, and a visual dashboard for moderators. Presented at TikTok’s San Jose office and won runner-up ($3,000) at the Future of Data Hackathon.

EXPERIMENT 03

AI + AGRICULTURE =

Soil Sentry helps farmers reduce water waste using solar sensors and an AI-powered app. It gives real-time moisture data and smart irrigation advice via chatbot. The project won 1st place at the GreenTech Challenge.

EXPERIMENT 04

AI + DATA PRIVACY =

Iris is an AI-powered moderation tool designed to protect user privacy by detecting personally identifiable information (PII) in videos before they go live. It addresses a major gap in content moderation, where sensitive data is often removed too late—after it's already exposed.
